How to use a restock watchlist

  1. Choose a short list of retailers that ship to you.
  2. List specific Pokémon TCG products or sets rather than enabling every notification.
  3. Set a price ceiling so a fast alert does not become an impulsive purchase.
  4. Review the sheet after a week and remove alert sources that do not help.

What this does not do

This is a planning resource. It does not provide product availability, retailer access, guaranteed alerts, or a guarantee that a purchase will succeed.
